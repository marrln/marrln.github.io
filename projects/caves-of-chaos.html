Cave Generation & Enemy AI

Cave Generation: The dungeon is built using a cellular automata algorithm to create organic, cave‑like layouts. Rooms and corridors are then connected with guarantee of full traversability via flood‑fill validation. Each level increases in size and difficulty.

Enemy AI: Monsters use A* pathfinding to navigate around walls and obstacles. Their behavior adapts based on the player's health and distance – ranged enemies will kite, while melee foes charge aggressively. Boss enemies have unique attack patterns.
